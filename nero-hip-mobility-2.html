A few years ago I suffered an ACL rupture — one of the worst injuries an athlete can face. After surgery I rebuilt my hip mobility completely from scratch. This guide is built from everything I learned through that process.

Stick to this 3-day program and you'll experience:

✓ Improved hip mobility & flexibility for better kicks
✓ Increased hip strength for power and control
✓ Greater range of motion and reduced stiffness
Before Every Session
WARM UP

Your body needs to be warm before stretching — this increases circulation, prepares your muscles, and reduces injury risk. Ideal after training, but if you're doing this standalone, run through the following first (2 sets of 10–15 reps each):

🦵 Leg Swings
🏃 Jogging in Place
⬇️ Bodyweight Squats
🚶 5 Min Brisk Walk
🥋 Or simply stretch right after your martial arts training
